# Security

Whalyon holds business owners' answers, scores and contact details. Please report anything on [whalyon.com](https://whalyon.com) that could let someone see another person's answers or report card, sign in as someone else, change a payment, or make the AI reading follow instructions hidden in text a user typed.

**Report privately** through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" on this repository, or by email to bjafaribox@gmail.com. Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

What is in scope:

- whalyon.com and its sign-in (phone, Google, LinkedIn);
- report cards, share cards and public badge pages showing more than their owner chose to share;
- payments and invoices;
- the AI reading of a report card.

What to include: the page or request, what you did, what you saw, and what you expected.

Please test only with your own account and do not access, change or delete other people's data.

This repository has no source code; it is Whalyon's public page.
